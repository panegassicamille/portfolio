Pause Café

Object designPersonal project · 2026

How do you turn a simple coffee into a real break?

Discover the project

At the counter, coffee is gulped down standing, eyes on the screen. Pause Café puts it back in your hands: a ceramic and stainless-steel totem that you assemble, grind and pour yourself, taking the time to truly stop.

The observation

At the office as at the bistro, a break is no longer a break: we eat in front of a screen, one eye on the clock, our mind already on the rest of the day.

  • Claire likes doing things herself
  • Luca never really switches off
  • Justine eats in front of a screen
  • Titouan feels guilty about stopping
  • Martine really takes her time

Why shouldn't Claire, Justine, Luca and Titouan have a share of Martine's life?

The lead

To truly unplug, the mind needs active rest: an activity that keeps the hands busy, without tiring them.

What the object must do

  1. 1. An intuitive gesture

    Grinding and assembling, without ever turning it into a puzzle.

  2. 2. Modular

    Separate parts that go quickly into the dishwasher.

  3. 3. Bistro materials

    White ceramic and stainless steel, for a new silhouette.

  4. 4. Filter time

    Filter coffee imposes a real pause.

The totem

  • White ceramic, the bistro's tableware
  • Stainless steel, the coffee-shop spirit

The ritual

  1. 1

    Grind. The ground coffee falls straight into the filter.

  2. 2

    Remove the grinder. The filter stays in place, on the handle.

  3. 3

    Pour the water. And wait for the coffee to filter through.

  4. 4

    Serve. With its dedicated handle, for yourself and for others.

It comes apart and stacks on the bistro's shelves.
