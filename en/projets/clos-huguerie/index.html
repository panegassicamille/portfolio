How do you turn the intention of an atmosphere into drawings every craftsman can build?

Hospitality · Bordeaux, Golden Triangle · 2024

Le Clos Huguerie

In the heart of Bordeaux's Golden Triangle, a townhouse becomes a boutique hotel. Studio Aurélien Lespinas set its atmosphere: the Bordeaux townhouse on the street side, the spirit of the Arcachon Bay on the courtyard side. During my internship, I turned this intention into plans, details and renderings for the tender package.

Internship at Studio Aurélien Lespinas

The place

A Bordeaux townhouse, two worlds

On rue Huguerie, a stone's throw from the Quinconces, a townhouse in pale stone, with wrought-iron balconies and a hidden garden, becomes a boutique hotel.

The studio's concept splits it into two worlds. On the street side, the rooms of the main house play on the codes of the Bordeaux townhouse. On the courtyard side, the garden-level rooms draw on the cabins of the Arcachon Bay.

My role

Making the atmosphere buildable

The art direction was set by the studio. My mission was to make it buildable, room by room.

I modelled the spaces in 3ds Max and retouched the renderings in Photoshop, then drew in AutoCAD the plans, elevations and joinery details of the tender package. Every moulding, every headboard, every colour is referenced in it, so that the joiner, the painter or the marble mason knows exactly what to make.

Street side, the intention

The Bordeaux of mouldings

In the street-side rooms, everything recalls the Bordeaux townhouse: moulded wall frames, architraves, panelled doors and brass handles.

The headboard draws diamonds over a striped paper, and the wardrobe is lined with a floral pattern.

Street side, the drawings

Every moulding dimensioned

On the drawings, the intention becomes an instruction. Each moulding is named by its profile (Guimier no. 200 for the architraves, no. 287 for the frames), the headboard is dimensioned in elevation, section and plan, and the wardrobe is drawn in elevation.

The spirit of the Arcachon Bay

On the garden side, a change of scene. Brushed pine painted in sea green covers the walls like the cladding of a beach cabin, the stripes recall beach canvas, and rattan pendants soften the light.

The bar, the intention

A lounge beneath the arches
