How do you turn the intention of an atmosphere into drawings every craftsman can build?

Discover the project

In the heart of Bordeaux's Golden Triangle, a townhouse becomes a boutique hotel, with art direction by Studio Aurélien Lespinas. During my internship, I translated that intention into renderings, drawings and details for the tender package.

Internship at Studio Aurélien Lespinas

The place

A Bordeaux townhouse, two worlds

On rue Huguerie, near the Quinconces, a pale-stone townhouse becomes a boutique hotel. The studio sets two worlds side by side: on the street side, the codes of the Bordeaux townhouse; on the courtyard side, the spirit of the Arcachon Bay cabins.

My role

Making the atmosphere buildable

The art direction came from the studio; my job was to make it buildable. Renderings in 3ds Max and Photoshop, then plans, elevations and details in AutoCAD for the tender package: every moulding and every colour is referenced for the craftsmen.

On the street side, everything recalls the Bordeaux townhouse: moulded frames, panelled doors, brass handles. The headboard draws diamonds over striped wallpaper.
