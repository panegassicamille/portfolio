How do you make a shop feel warm without redoing everything?

Retail · Oftringen, Switzerland · 2026

Christ

« Sparkle Lounges »: a jewellery shop that welcomes you like a living room, where everyone goes straight to what they came for. Pilot store in Oftringen, Switzerland.

Project carried out at CBA Design

The starting point

Christ, a Swiss jeweller and watchmaker, wanted to bring more warmth to its shops, which until then had been very minimal: to make people want to come in, welcome Gen Z while keeping its loyal customers, showcase its own collections and develop its services.

With one constraint: keeping part of the existing furniture.

  1. Warm up

    Warm light, stained oak, curtains.

  2. Bring together

    Seats and tables where you try things on together.

  3. Inspire

    Jewellery shown as looks, like in a magazine.

Christ's collections at the centre

In felt pen on the plan, a first option divided the shop by category — fine jewellery, fashion, watches. The option chosen gives the space to the Christ collections, with the LookBook at the centre; partner brands move to the side walls.

Keep the furniture, change how it reads

The existing wall units are kept and re-clad. Velvet-lined niches, a stained-oak band and a copper plinth for the Christ collections; one velvet colour per category — sage green for fine jewellery, light orange for fashion.

Depending on what you came for

No set route: in a jewellery shop, you go straight to what you need. Each need has its own space, recognisable by its atmosphere — rug, curtain, pendant lights.

  • Get inspired → The LookBook
  • Try on, mix and match → The Sparkle table
  • A service, some advice → The Christ's Desk
  • Getting pierced → The piercing suite
  • Choosing an engagement ring → Diamonds & Love

Get inspired

The LookBook

At the centre, looks built around statement pieces and the season's selection: you no longer look for a ring, you find a style.

Try on, mix and match

The Sparkle table

A modular table where you sit down to try pieces on in front of a mirror. It hosts the Gen Z collections, a seasonal collection or a charms bar.

A service, some advice

The Christ's Desk

Till, watch after-sales service and gift wrapping, brought together in a single counter.

Getting pierced

The piercing suite

A closed booth in stained oak, with its mirror and display: piercing happens away from prying eyes.

Choosing an engagement ring

Diamonds & Love

At the back, near the bridal and diamond collections, a green velvet lounge where you take your time, seated.
