RetailOftringen, Switzerland · 2026

How do you make a shop feel warm without redoing everything?

Discover the project

A jeweller's that welcomes you like a living room: you move from one experience to the next and take the time to stay. Pilot store in Oftringen, Switzerland.

Project carried out at CBA Design

The starting point

Christ, a Swiss jeweller and watchmaker, wanted to warm up its shops: all white and bright light, this one felt cold, almost clinical, and the brand's own collections barely stood out.

The challenge: win over Gen Z without losing loyal customers, and showcase collections and services while keeping part of the furniture.

  1. Warm up

    Warm light, stained oak, curtains.

  2. Bring together

    Seats and tables where you try things on together.

  3. Inspire

    Jewellery shown as looks, like in a magazine.

The first intentions

Three mood boards, one per intention: welcoming, bringing together, inspiring. The first concept sketch follows from them.

Christ's collections at the centre

A first option divided the shop by category. The chosen option puts the Christ collections at the centre, around the LookBook, and the partner brands on the walls.

Keep the furniture, change how it reads

Rather than redoing everything, we keep the white shell and warm it up. The existing wall units are re-clad: velvet-lined niches, stained oak, a copper plinth, one velvet colour per category.

Depending on what you came for

No set route: you move freely from one space to another and take the time to linger. Each need has its own space, recognisable by its atmosphere.

  • Get inspired → The LookBook
  • Try on, mix and match → The Sparkle table
  • A service, some advice → The Christ's Desk
  • Getting pierced → The piercing suite
  • Choosing an engagement ring → Diamonds & Love
