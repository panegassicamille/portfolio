Cristal Bar · Graphic design

How do you turn an evening neighbourhood bar into an essential place to be, open from morning to night?

Graphic design · Paris 15th arrondissement · 2025

Cristal Bar

Le Cristal, a neighbourhood bar in Paris's 15th arrondissement, wanted to open up to the daytime without losing its festive spirit. A visual identity in two moods: the energy of the evening, the softness of breaks — tied together by handwritten lettering and a series of illustrations.

Freelance project

The context

An evening institution

In the heart of Paris's 15th arrondissement, Le Cristal has become an essential neighbourhood spot: lively nights, the buzz of live matches, generous smash burgers, and a loyal community.

Building on this night-time success, the bar wanted to open up to the daytime, with a fresher, lighter offer.

The challenge

From morning to night, without losing its soul

The aim was to widen the audience — workers looking to switch off, end-of-day sports lovers — while preserving the warm, friendly DNA of the place.

How do you design an identity that embodies both the festive energy of the evening and the bright softness of daytime breaks?

The universe

A palette for day and night

A deep olive green for the bar spirit, a sunny yellow and a fuchsia pink for energy, a warm beige for the softness of breaks.

Two typefaces: Dancing Script, handwritten, for warmth; Poppins, clean, for legibility.

The logotype

Lettering that breathes

A fluid, organic handwritten lettering. Its thick and thin strokes evoke the indulgence of smash burgers as much as the softness of afternoon sunlight; its curves echo today's coffee shops.

Below it, « rive gauche » in lowercase, lighter and airier: it anchors the bar in its neighbourhood, with understated chic.

The monogram

A « C » that goes everywhere

Clean and striking, the monogram picks up the same enveloping roundness. Circled by « cristal rive gauche », it adapts to every medium — textiles, signage, social media — to be recognised at a glance.

The illustrations

A day at Le Cristal

To tell the story of the place at every hour, a series of illustrations in a simple, spontaneous black line: people work there, cycle past, nap, meet up after work over a beer, a pitcher or a burger.

The same line from morning to night: that is what connects the two faces of Le Cristal.

A day at Le Cristal

The roll-out

From the T-shirt to the terrace

Today the graphic system lives across the whole Cristal ecosystem: outdoor signage, staff T-shirts, limited editions and social media.
