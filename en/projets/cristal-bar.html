Cristal Bar

Graphic designParis 15th arrondissement · 2025

How do you turn an evening neighbourhood bar into an essential place to be, open from morning to night?

Discover the project

A neighbourhood bar in the 15th arrondissement opening up to the daytime without losing its festive spirit: a two-tempo identity, tied together by hand lettering and illustrations.

Freelance project

The context

An evening institution

Lively evenings, matches on screen, generous smash burgers: the Cristal is a must in the 15th. Building on that success, it wanted to open up to the daytime.

The challenge

From morning to night, without losing its soul

The aim was to attract workers looking to switch off and after-work athletes, without losing the place's warm DNA.

How do you embody both the energy of the evening and the gentleness of daytime breaks?

The universe

A palette for day and night

An olive green for the bar spirit, a yellow and a fuchsia pink for energy, a warm beige for the breaks. Dancing Script for warmth, Poppins for legibility.

The logotype

Lettering that breathes

Hand lettering, fluid and indulgent, echoing today's coffee shops. Below it, « rive gauche » in lowercase anchors the bar in its neighbourhood.

The monogram

A « C » that goes everywhere

The monogram has the same roundness. Circled by « cristal rive gauche », it goes everywhere, from textiles to social media, recognisable at a glance.

The illustrations

A day at Le Cristal

A series of spontaneous black-line illustrations tells the story of the place at every hour: working, cycling past, napping, meeting up after work. The same line ties the Cristal's two faces together.

From the T-shirt to the terrace
