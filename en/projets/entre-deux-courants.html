Between Currents

HospitalityAirport lounge · 2026

Discover the project

A premium lounge in an international airport: a place to slow down between two flights, designed like a gently flowing river.

Project carried out at CBA Design

The context

Lounges that all look alike

In airports, lounges all look alike: you wait there without ever really settling in. Travellers, often in a hurry, are above all looking for calm.

The concept

Between currents

The lounge draws on the river that runs through the city, a slow and peaceful current. You leave the flow of the airport behind, and time slows down.

The plan

Many ways to wait

Everyone waits in their own way: eating, working, resting, meeting up. The lounge is divided into worlds, each with its own atmosphere.

The reception

Step in, slow down

As soon as you enter, you leave the bustle of the terminal behind. The curves of the reception set the tone for the whole lounge.

The bar and the lounge

The heart of the place

The liveliest corner of the lounge: people meet over a drink or watch a game before heading off again.

The furniture

Made-to-measure banquettes

Designed for the project, they change with each use: enveloping for privacy, more open for sharing a meal.

The dining room

Under a watchmaker's ceiling

Sitting down to a real meal before the flight, away from the comings and goings. On the ceiling, rays inspired by watch mechanisms: a nod to Switzerland, where the airport is.

Work, rest

To each their own calm

Some work until the last minute, others want to sleep. Two separate areas, so that everyone finds their own calm.
